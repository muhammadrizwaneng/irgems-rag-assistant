SHORT INTRODUCTION
==================================================

IRGEMS is a Pakistan-based online jewelry store offering stylish, durable, and affordable jewelry for everyday wear and special occasions. We believe luxury should be accessible, durable, and effortless.

At IRGEMS, we curate premium artificial, zircon, and anti-tarnish jewelry designed to complement every look—from simple daily outfits to weddings, dinners, and celebrations. Our focus is lasting shine, comfortable wear, and modern designs that feel elevated without the high price of traditional precious-metal jewelry.

Shop online at: https://irgems.pk

WHAT KIND OF JEWELRY WE SELL
==================================================

IRGEMS specializes in fashion and daily-wear jewelry, including:

- Rings (adjustable open rings, designer-inspired rings, zircon statement rings, heart rings, and more)
- Bangles & Bracelets (including minimalist gold-tone pieces like ball-chain bracelets)
- Earrings
- Nail rings
- Necklaces & jewelry sets

Materials and finishes you will commonly find:
- High-quality stainless steel
- High-grade titanium steel with 18K real gold PVD plating
- Zircon / cubic zirconia accents
- Anti-tarnish and waterproof daily-wear designs
- Hypoallergenic options that are nickel-free and lead-free on selected pieces

Many products are sold as single pieces, and several rings feature adjustable fits so customers do not need to worry about exact sizing.

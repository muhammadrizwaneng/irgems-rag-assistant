IRGEMS Jewelry Care Guide
Website: https://irgems.pk

IRGEMS jewelry is crafted for everyday wear using premium materials such as stainless steel and high-grade titanium steel with 18K real gold PVD plating, often finished with zircon accents. With simple care, your pieces stay bright, comfortable, and long-lasting.

==================================================
HOW TO CLEAN STAINLESS STEEL & TITANIUM JEWELRY
==================================================

1. Basic daily clean
- After wearing, wipe your jewelry gently with a soft, dry microfiber or cotton cloth.
- This removes skin oils, dust, and light residue and helps keep the shine.

2. Deeper clean (when needed)
- Mix warm water with a small amount of mild soap (gentle hand wash or dish soap is fine).
- Dip a soft cloth or soft toothbrush in the soapy water and gently clean the surface.
- For zircon or stone settings, brush lightly around the stones to remove trapped dirt.
- Rinse briefly with clean water if needed.
- Dry thoroughly with a soft cloth before storing or wearing again.
- Never put jewelry away while wet.

3. Extra shine tip
- Buff lightly with a clean, dry soft cloth after cleaning.
- Avoid abrasive pads, baking soda pastes, toothpaste, or harsh polishing compounds—these can scratch the finish or dull PVD plating.

==================================================
WHAT TO AVOID (PERFUME, CHEMICALS, ETC.)
==================================================

Even though many IRGEMS pieces are waterproof, sweatproof, anti-tarnish, and built for daily wear, harsh chemicals can still damage plating, stones, and finishes over time.

Avoid direct contact with:
- Perfume, cologne, and body sprays
- Hairspray, hair gel, and styling products
- Lotions, creams, and sunscreen
- Household cleaners, bleach, detergents, and chlorine
- Hand sanitizer in large amounts (apply sanitizer first, let it dry, then put jewelry on)
- Chlorinated pools and strong spa chemicals when possible (especially for plated or stone-set pieces)

Best habit:
- Put jewelry on last—after makeup, perfume, lotion, and hairspray.
- Take jewelry off before cleaning the house or applying strong chemicals.

Also avoid:
- Dropping pieces on hard surfaces
- Rough scrubbing or sharp tools near stone settings
- Ultrasonic cleaners or chemical jewelry dips unless the product page specifically allows them

==================================================
HOW TO STORE THEM PROPERLY
==================================================

Proper storage prevents scratches, tangles, and finish wear.

Do:
- Store each piece in a soft pouch, airtight jewelry bag, or a lined jewelry box.
- Keep pieces separated so rings, bracelets, and chains do not rub against each other.
- Store in a cool, dry place away from humidity and direct sunlight.
- Close clasps on necklaces and bracelets before storing to reduce tangling.
- Keep original packaging when possible—it is useful for gifting, travel, and returns/exchanges.

Don’t:
- Leave jewelry in bathrooms where steam and moisture build up.
- Toss multiple pieces loosely into one bag or drawer.
- Store wet jewelry.
- Expose pieces to extreme heat for long periods.
